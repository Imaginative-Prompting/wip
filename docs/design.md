# Design

WIP is a quiet work surface: a large list, readable media, and one persistent player.

Use a monochrome canvas and warm neutral text. Hierarchy comes from spacing,
alignment, size, and weight. Do not wrap entries, tags, search, or the current cut
in cards, borders, pills, or tinted panels. Backgrounds belong only to overlays
that must obscure content and to the primary playback control. Keep labels in
sentence case and remove decorative slogans and explanatory filler.

Hover states brighten text, underline a filter, or make a small deliberate icon
movement. Keep keyboard focus visible, and respect reduced motion. Retain a
single bottom player across navigation and filtering. Fullscreen exit stays at
the bottom right.
